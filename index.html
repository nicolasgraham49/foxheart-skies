Land, sea, mountains and towns away from the hand-made airports are procedural: land is where the real airports are, mountains follow rough relief hints, and every airport you pass is built from its real runway data. Shift N in flight flies direct to the nearest or chosen destination.

Failures and emergenciesRandom failure during a flight
“Trigger now” works in flight (pause, then Settings). Engine failure at V1 arms for the next takeoff. The first officer calls the checklist; press J (or A on a controller) for the next action, and declare MAYDAY or PAN-PAN from the radio (T / X).
